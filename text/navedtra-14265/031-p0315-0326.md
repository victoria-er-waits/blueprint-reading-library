POTASSIUM CHROMATE—KCr, reagent, used in
testing for chlorine levels in water.
POTASSIUM HYDROXIDE—(Caustic Potash)
K2CrO7, a white powder, strongly basic
(alkaline), when dissolved in water produces
heat. Used to balance water pH and absorb
chlorine. Also used as a reagent to test water
salinity.
SILVER NITRATE—AgNO3, reagent, used to
determine amount of salinity and chloride in
water.
SODIUM CARBONATE—(Soda ash), Na2CO3, salt
of carbonic acid, strongly basic (alkaline).
Used in water softening and balancing water
pH to aid coagulation.
SODIUM ETHYLENE—(EDTA), Na2CH3CH2,
reagent, used in solution with Diaminete-
tracetate to detect minerals which cause
hardness in water.
SODIUM HYDROXIDE—(Caustic Soda) NaOH, a
strong base (alkaline), white powder used to
balance pH in water to aid coagulation, and
absorb chlorine.
SODIUM HYPOCHLORITE—NaOCl, a salt usually
furnished in solution, used for disinfection of
water.
SULFURIC ACID—(Standard), H2SO4, strong acid,
used to balance water pH and aid in coagulation.
THIOSULFATE—A salt, used to neutralize chlorine
water. Used to sterilize water sample
