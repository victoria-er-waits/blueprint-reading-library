Residential example plan set

American Farmhouse: A Design

Jay Osborne · freefarmhouse.com · 2020 · 17 of 25 sheets shown

A complete small-house architectural set: cover with sheet index, symbols legend and abbreviations, general notes, site, floor, roof and RCP/electrical plans, elevations, sections, details and door/window/fixture schedules. Imperial scales (1/4"=1'-0" plans). Good for learning title blocks, sheet numbering, callouts and schedules. See residential vs municipal civil drawings.

Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0, by Jay Osborne. This excerpt (17 sheets, plus page images and extracted text) is a modified version shared under the same licence. 8 sheets are held back for internal testing. Full set: freefarmhouse.com
