# How the Blueprint Reader should use: Mechanical Drawing: A Practical Manual of Self-Instruction (1908, public domain)

Source: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/pdf/11-Mechanical-Drawing-Self-Instruction-1908.pdf
Book id: `mechanical-drawing-1908`. Full rights note: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/catalog.md

## What it teaches
Deep background on drafting: instruments, geometric constructions, orthographic projection, shade lines, lettering, tracing and blueprint reproduction. Explains WHY drawings look the way they do.

## Order-of-operations steps it supports

## What to skip
Instruments and materials (PDF pp. 13-56) and the publisher's ads at the back (PDF p. 179 on).

## Pitfalls
- 1908 conventions are outdated: shade lines, first-angle examples and hand-inking practices are not used on modern CAD sheets.
- Text is Internet Archive OCR of a scan; expect errors and confirm against the page image.
- Mechanical parts, not civil.
- Never quote this book as approval or code for a BC project. The project drawings, specs and legend govern.

## Practice prompts
1. Using Part III, explain how the three views on sheet X relate (which is plan, front, side).
2. Explain one convention in this 1908 book that modern drawings no longer use, and what replaced it.
