# How the Blueprint Reader should use: Construction Print Reading in the Field, TM 5-704 (US Army/Air Force 1969)

Source: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/pdf/03-TM-5-704-Construction-Print-Reading.pdf
Book id: `tm-5-704`. Full rights note: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/catalog.md

## What it teaches
Construction (not machine) print reading: principles, architectural working drawings, utilities (water supply and distribution, sewer, electrical), heating and refrigeration plans, machine drawings and bills of materials.

## Order-of-operations steps it supports

- Step 7: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/guide/steps/step-7-cross-checks.md

| Task | Read |
|---|---|

| Heating/refrigeration | Ch 4, chunks 013-014 (pp. 145-168) |
| Bills of materials (quantity cross-check) | Ch 5-6, chunks 015-016+ (pp. 169-192) |

Page numbers are PDF page numbers (as in index.json), not printed page numbers.

## What to skip
Chapter boundaries above are by text chunk and approximate; open the PDF page to confirm.

## Pitfalls
- Text is OCR of a 1969 scan, so numbers can be wrong. Always confirm against the PDF page.
- 1969 US military practice and materials; not current BC code.
- Never quote this book as approval or code for a BC project. The project drawings, specs and legend govern.

## Practice prompts
1. Using TM 5-704 Ch 3, explain how a water distribution plan shows pipe size, material and valves, then apply it to sheet X.
2. Using Ch 5-6, check the bill of materials on sheet X against the items shown on the plan.
