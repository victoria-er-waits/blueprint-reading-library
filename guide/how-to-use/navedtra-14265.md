# How the Blueprint Reader should use: Utilitiesman Basic, NAVEDTRA 14265 (US Navy)

Source: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/pdf/05-NAVEDTRA-14265-Utilitiesman-Basic.pdf
Book id: `navedtra-14265`. Full rights note: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/catalog.md

## What it teaches
Utilities trade knowledge: plans, specs and colour coding, plumbing, valves and accessories, fixtures, pumps and compressors, water treatment.

## Order-of-operations steps it supports
- Step 4: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/guide/steps/step-4-notes-legend.md
- Step 5: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/guide/steps/step-5-one-sheet-at-a-time.md
- Step 6: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/guide/steps/step-6-sanity-check.md

| Task | Read |

| Plumbing layout and fittings | Ch 3, pp. 71-122 |
| Valves (gate, butterfly, check, PRV, air release) | Ch 4, pp. 123-154 |
| Pumps | Ch 6, pp. 197-250 |
| Water treatment | Ch 7, pp. 251-278 |

Page numbers are PDF page numbers (as in index.json), not printed page numbers.

## What to skip
Ch 2 advanced-base components and Ch 8 equipment maintenance for tender reading.

## Pitfalls
- Military field practice; municipal valve chambers follow MMCD and the city standard drawing.
- Imperial pipe sizes; BC drawings mix mm and nominal inch sizes.
- Never quote this book as approval or code for a BC project. The project drawings, specs and legend govern.

## Practice prompts
1. Using Ch 4, identify each valve in the valve chamber detail on sheet X and its purpose.
2. Using Ch 6, list the pump data a tender sheet should show and check sheet X for each.
