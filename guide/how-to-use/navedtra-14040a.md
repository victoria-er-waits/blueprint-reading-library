# How the Blueprint Reader should use: Blueprint Reading and Sketching, NAVEDTRA 14040A (US Navy 2015)

Source: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/pdf/02-NAVEDTRA-14040A-Blueprint-Reading-and-Sketching.pdf
Book id: `navedtra-14040a`. Full rights note: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/catalog.md

## What it teaches
The classic all-trades primer: parts of a blueprint and title block, projections and views, machine drawings, piping, electrical, architectural and structural steel prints. Glossary and symbol appendices.

| Electrical symbols | Ch 6, pp. 108-137 and Appendix III, pp. 222-231 |
| Architectural and structural steel | Ch 7, pp. 138-174 |
| Unknown term | Appendix I Glossary, pp. 209-215 |

Page numbers are PDF page numbers (as in index.json), not printed page numbers.

## What to skip
Ch 8 Developments and Intersections (sheet-metal layout) and Appendix II aircraft hydraulic symbols unless relevant. Assignment pages 238-265 are quizzes.

## Pitfalls
- US Navy practice and US symbols (ANSI/MIL). BC municipal sets follow MMCD and the city standard; the project legend always wins.
- Imperial units throughout.
- Never quote this book as approval or code for a BC project. The project drawings, specs and legend govern.

## Practice prompts
1. Using NAVEDTRA 14040A Ch 1, list every field in the title block of sheet X and what each one means.
2. Using Ch 5 and the piping symbols, name each valve and fitting on detail X.
3. Using Ch 7, read the steel member callouts on sheet X and give size and spacing.
