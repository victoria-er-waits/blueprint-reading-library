# How the Blueprint Reader should use: Control and Topographic Surveying, EM 1110-1-1005 (USACE 2007)

Source: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/pdf/10-USACE-EM-1110-1-1005-Control-Topographic-Surveying.pdf
Book id: `usace-em-1110-1-1005`. Full rights note: https://victoria-er-waits.github.io/blueprint-reading-library/catalog.md

## What it teaches
Survey control, accuracy standards, datums and coordinate systems, topo survey methods, and how site plans and survey maps are produced.

Page numbers are PDF page numbers (as in index.json), not printed page numbers.

## What to skip
GPS RTK, laser scanning and contracting chapters (Ch 7-10, 13) unless the task is about survey methods.

## Pitfalls
- US datums (NAVD88, State Plane). BC uses CGVD2013/CGVD28 and UTM/NAD83(CSRS). Check the datum note on the drawing.
- Imperial/US survey feet in places.
- Never quote this book as approval or code for a BC project. The project drawings, specs and legend govern.

## Practice prompts
1. Using Ch 5, find the horizontal and vertical datum on survey sheet X and report it, or mark it "needs check".
2. Using Ch 11, read the benchmark and contour information on sheet X.
