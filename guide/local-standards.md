# Local and civil standards catalog (checked 2026-10-12)

Use this when a drawing set says "MMCD", "Supplementary Standard Detail Drawing (SSD)", or cites a city bylaw schedule. BC municipal tenders are built on the MMCD; each city then adopts, replaces or deletes MMCD details in its own supplement. When a sheet says "as per MMCD W3" or "SD W4", look up which version that city uses before reading the detail.

Classes: **HOST** = public domain / open licence, copy kept here. **LINK-ONLY** = free to view at the source, but no licence allowing redistribution was found (or redistribution is barred). **SKIP** = paid or restricted.
